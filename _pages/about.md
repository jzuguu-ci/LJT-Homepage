Hello! I’m Junteng Liu, a first-year PhD candidate in the HKUST NLP Group at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology. My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning, with interests in LLM reasoning and reinforcement learning, truthfulness and interpretability, and hallucination in vision-language models.
